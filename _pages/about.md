I am currently a Ph.D. candidate at the [State Key Laboratory of Virtual Reality Technology and Systems](https://scse.buaa.edu.cn/), School of Computer Science and Engineering (SCSE), Beihang University, advised by Prof. [Shi-Min Hu](https://cg.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/shimin.htm). I am also a visiting student at the [Graphics and Geometric Computing Group](https://cg.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/), Tsinghua University, where I am a core developer of the [Jittor framework](https://github.com/Jittor/jittor). I have served as a reviewer for top-tier conferences and journals, including *ICLR*, *ICML*, *IEEE Transactions on Image Processing*, and *Computational Visual Media Journal*.
